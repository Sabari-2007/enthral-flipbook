Key features

Everything you need to turn plain PDFs into flipbooks people actually enjoy reading.

Unique design

Make each flipbook a clear extension of your brand — add a clickable logo, customize the direct link to your flipbooks, set the background and the color palette to reflect your company style.

Easy sharing

With FlipBook, your PDFs turn into links you can share anywhere: email them directly, post to socials, or embed into your websites. Share single flipbooks or entire collections.

Content analytics
